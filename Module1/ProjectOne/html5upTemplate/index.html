BTS as a Group

BTS is a seven member South Korean boy group. The group consist of RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook. BTS debuted June 13, 2013 with 4 vocalist and 3 rappers under the company called BigHit entertainment. Their first album was titled 2 Cool 4 Skool with the title track named "No More Dream." BTS discography now has over 300 songs. Their songs talk about many things like troubles of school life, mental health, coming of age, loss, journey towards self-love, individualism, and the consequences of fame and recognition.

RM is the leader of the group. He is the fourth oldest and one of the rappers, producers, lyricist, and composers for their songs. Jin is the oldest in their group. He is a vocalist and songwriter known for his breath control and strong falsetto which made people refer to him as the "silver voice." Suga is second oldest in the group. Like RM, he's one of the rappers, producers, lyricist, and composers for their songs. However he also produces for other people as well. J-Hope is the third oldest. He also is one of the rappers, producers, lyricist, and composers. Additionally, he's known for his dancing skills and is the dance leader. Jimin is the third youngest. He is a vocalist, songwriter, and main dancer in the group. He spent the least amount of time as a trainee. V is the second youngest. He is a vocalist and songwriter known for his deep baritone voice. Jungkook is the youngest. He is a vocalist, songwriter, and main dancer. Known as the golden maknae for being able to do anything he puts his mind to.

Future

Coming out soon is the release of BTS's 5th Full Album called Arirang. The new album holds special significance as it marks the first album released by the group in three years and nine months, while also indicating the direction the group will take going forward. The members were deeply involved in the creation of the songs, infusing their own thoughts and colors into them while musically expressing the emotions and struggles they have experienced along their journey. The album features 14 tracks and is coming out March 20, 2026.
